Nutrition Support

Disease-Based Diet Chart

Simple nutrition guidance for common health conditions. These are general wellness recommendations and should not replace personal medical advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Cold / Flu

Recommended: warm soups, herbal tea, lemon water, rice, oats, fruits like oranges and berries, and light meals.

  • Drink plenty of fluids to stay hydrated.
  • Eat small, easy-to-digest meals.
  • Include vitamin-rich fruits and vegetables.

Avoid: heavy fried foods, very spicy meals, and excessive sugar.

Gastrointestinal Infection

Recommended: oral rehydration solution, broth, bananas, rice, applesauce, toast, yogurt if tolerated.

  • Take frequent small sips of water.
  • Choose bland, gentle foods first.
  • Restore hydration before heavy meals.

Avoid: oily, spicy, fried, and dairy-heavy foods until symptoms improve.

Allergy Symptoms

Recommended: fresh fruits, vegetables, hydration-focused meals, whole grains, and foods without known triggers.

  • Maintain a balanced routine with anti-inflammatory foods.
  • Drink enough water throughout the day.
  • Identify and avoid personal trigger foods.

Avoid: known allergens and highly processed foods with additives you react to.

Dehydration / Fluid Loss

Recommended: water, oral rehydration fluids, coconut water, soups, watermelon, bananas, potatoes.

  • Take small, frequent sips instead of large amounts at once.
  • Include light starchy foods to restore energy.
  • Focus on hydration before reintroducing heavier meals.

Avoid: alcohol, excess caffeine, and overly salty snacks until you recover.

General Recovery

Recommended: oats, rice, eggs, soft proteins, berries, citrus fruits, and vegetables with easy digestion.

  • Eat smaller meals throughout the day.
  • Balance protein, fiber, and fluids.
  • Keep the diet simple and nutrient-rich.

Avoid: skipping meals, overly greasy foods, and heavy meals while unwell.

Healthy Daily Habit

Recommended: a mix of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, protein, and enough clean water every day.

  • Keep meals regular and balanced.
  • Choose natural foods over highly processed options.
  • Adjust portion sizes to your energy needs and health condition.

Avoid: long gaps without food, excess sugar, and very low fluid intake.

Important note

This page provides general educational nutrition guidance. If symptoms are severe, persistent, or worsening, speak to a qualified doctor or healthcare professional.
