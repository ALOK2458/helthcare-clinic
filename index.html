Nutrition Support

Diet Chart

Simple food guidance based on common health conditions.

Cold / Flu Warm soups, herbal tea, fruits, broth, fluids, and light meals help support hydration and recovery.
Gastrointestinal issues Plain rice, bananas, toast, yogurt, broths, and oral rehydration fluids are often easier to tolerate.
Allergy symptoms Fresh fruits, vegetables, and plenty of water help maintain energy while avoiding trigger foods.
Dehydration Electrolyte drinks, coconut water, soups, and water-rich foods can help replace fluid and mineral loss.
